Local image matching

Compares on-screen images with perceptual fingerprints of reference images you add below (for example official key art). Runs entirely on this computer. Needs the optional "read data on all sites" permission so images can be re-read from their original host (normally from cache). It recognises re-posts of your reference images – it cannot recognise arbitrary GTA 6 screenshots, and it will miss heavily cropped or edited images.

Privacy & limitations

Privacy. SpoilerShield has no accounts, analytics, ads or remote code. Page text is analysed inside the tab and never leaves your computer. Settings and simple counters (number of items hidden per day – no URLs, titles or sites) are stored with chrome.storage.local.

Permissions. storage saves your settings. The content script runs on all sites because spoilers can appear anywhere; it only reads the page to decide what to hide. The optional all-sites host permission is requested only if you turn on local image matching.

Limitations. No browser extension can guarantee that every spoiler is hidden before it is displayed. Content can slip through when it is rendered in a canvas or video frame, inside closed shadow DOM, inside cross-origin frames the extension cannot access, in pages opened before the extension was installed, on browser-internal pages (chrome://, the Web Store), in images without descriptive text, in audio, or when websites change their markup. Detection is keyword-and-context based: unusual phrasing, other languages, or heavy obfuscation may be missed, and harmless content may occasionally be hidden.
